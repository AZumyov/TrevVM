# Contributor Covenant of Conduct🥰

## Our Pledge🖖

We as members, contributors, and maintainers of TrevVM pledge to make participation in this project a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, background, identity, experience level, or point of view.

Users and contributors must respect school, workplace, network, system, and service policies, as well as applicable laws. The project must not be used to evade security monitoring, disrupt services, access data without permission, or interfere with another person’s systems or accounts.

## Expected Behavior🤞

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include:

- Being respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Giving and receiving feedback gracefully.
- Focusing on the issue or contribution rather than the person.
- Respecting different experiences, abilities, identities, and opinions.
- Clearly documenting reproduction steps, risks, and limitations.
- Reporting security vulnerabilities privately rather than publicly exposing them.

## Unacceptable Behavior🤏

The following behavior is not tolerated:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and deliberate disruption.
- Publishing someone else’s private information without permission.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Encouraging or documenting unauthorized access, credential theft, malware, evasion of security controls, or disruption of services.
- Submitting code or instructions intended to circumvent monitoring, or acceptable-use controls without permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate or unprofessional in a public technical community.

## Scope and Responsibilities🙀

This Code of Conduct applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, documentation, code reviews, chat, and project-related events. Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards and may remove, edit, or reject contributions that violate them.

Contributors are responsible for ensuring that their submissions are lawful, safe, and appropriately documented. Do not include credentials, and/or private data in issues, pull requests, or repository files.

## Reporting Issues✍️

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, report it privately to the repository maintainers through the contact method listed in the repository profile or README. Please include:

- A description of what happened.
- Relevant links, screenshots, or other evidence.
- The date and location of the incident.
- Any context that may help maintainers investigate.

Do not publicly disclose sensitive personal information or security details while reporting an incident. Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as reasonably possible.

## Enforcement🙌

Maintainers may take any action they consider appropriate, including removing content, requesting changes, issuing a warning, temporarily restricting participation, or permanently banning participation. Decisions will be made with the safety and well-being of the community in mind.

Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is not acceptable.
