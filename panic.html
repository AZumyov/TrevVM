TrevVM panic key

Press = while the desktop tab is focused to replace it with a neutral page. This runs in your local browser, not inside the VM.

Option 1: bookmarklet

Drag this link to your bookmarks bar, then click it once on the desktop tab:

Panic key

A bookmarklet runs only on the page where you click it. You need to click it again after a full page reload.

Option 2: userscript

Install Tampermonkey or Violentmonkey, then add panic.user.js. It runs automatically on localhost and Codespaces preview domains, including after reloads.

Change the destination

Edit s='https://www.google.com' in the bookmarklet or in panic.user.js. Any neutral page works.
